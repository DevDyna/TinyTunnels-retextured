Room Duplicator Mockups

Three ways to pay for and build a copy of a room in survival. All three start from a Room Blueprint of the room. The example is a Normal Machine (7×7×7 inside) that needs 214 more blocks than you have. Design notes: tiny-tunnels-room-duplicator.md.

Shared by all three: the Room Blueprint. Wrench a machine while holding paper in the other hand. You get a blueprint of the room as it is now; the room isn't touched.

Option A

Duplicator block with a checklist screen

A new block. Its screen lists every item the copy needs, with what you have and what's missing. It fills from items you put in and from chests, hoppers, pipes or tunnels next to it.

Flow

  1. Craft and place the Duplicator, with a chest next to it.
  2. Put in the blueprint and an empty machine of the same size.
  3. Fill the chest; the list turns green as items arrive.
  4. Press Build; the bound machine appears in the output slot.

For

  • The list is always in front of you, sorted by what's missing.
  • Works with automation: pipes and tunnels can feed it.

Against

  • Most work: a block, a screen, a menu, syncing the list.
  • One more block to craft.
Option B

Duplicator block, no screen

The same block without a screen. Right-click it to load the blueprint and machine; it pulls from chests next to it. What's missing shows in Jade and in chat. It builds by itself when complete.

Flow

  1. Place the Duplicator next to a chest.
  2. Right-click it with the blueprint, then with an empty machine.
  3. Look at it (Jade) or right-click it to see what's missing.
  4. When the chest has everything, it builds and drops the bound machine.

For

  • Much less work than A; still automatic.

Against

  • A long missing list is hard to read in a tooltip or chat.
  • No place to see the full list at once.
Option C

No new block: the wrench and your inventory

Wrench an empty machine with the blueprint in your other hand. If your inventory has everything, it's taken and the machine is bound to the copy. If not, the blueprint's tooltip and a chat message say what's missing.

Flow

  1. Hold the blueprint in your off hand, the wrench in your main hand.
  2. Hover the blueprint to see have / need from your inventory.
  3. Gather what's missing.
  4. Wrench an empty machine: the items are taken and it's bound to the copy.

For

  • Least work; nothing new to craft.

Against

  • Big rooms need far more than an inventory holds (a Maximum Machine is 13×13×13).
  • No automation.
